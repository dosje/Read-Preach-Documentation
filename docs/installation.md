# Installation

Read & Preach is available for **iPhone & iPad** and for **Android**.

## Requirements

| | iPhone & iPad | Android |
|---|---|---|
| Devices | iPhone and iPad | Phones and tablets |
| System | iOS 18.2 or later | Android 8.0 or later |
| Store | App Store | Google Play |

No internet connection is required after installation.

## iPhone & iPad

### Installing from the App Store

1. Open the [**App Store**](https://apps.apple.com/app/read-preach/id6760897523) on your iPhone or iPad
2. Search for **"Read & Preach"**
3. Tap **Get**, then authenticate with Face ID, Touch ID, or your Apple ID password
4. The app downloads and appears on your home screen

### Updating the app

1. Open the **App Store**
2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner
3. Scroll down to see available updates
4. Tap **Update** next to Read & Preach

Alternatively, enable automatic updates in **Settings → App Store → App Updates**.

### Uninstalling

Press and hold the Read & Preach icon on your home screen → tap **Remove App** → **Delete App**.

## Android

### Installing from Google Play

1. Open [**Google Play**](https://play.google.com/store/apps) on your phone or tablet
2. Search for **"Read & Preach"**
3. Tap **Install**
4. The app downloads and appears in your app drawer

### Updating the app

1. Open **Google Play**
2. Tap your profile icon in the top right corner → **Manage apps & device**
3. Tap **Updates available** and then **Update** next to Read & Preach

Google Play updates apps automatically by default.

### Uninstalling

Press and hold the Read & Preach icon → tap **Uninstall** (or drag it to **Uninstall**). You can also go to **Android Settings → Apps → Read & Preach → Uninstall**.

## Transferring data to a new device

Read & Preach stores all data locally. To move your data to a new device:

1. On your **old device**, open Read & Preach → **Settings → Export & Import** (on Android: **Settings → General → Export & Import**)
2. Tap **Export**, choose what to include, and save the file (e.g. to iCloud Drive, Files, Google Drive, or send it by email)
3. On your **new device**, install Read & Preach
4. Open **Settings → Export & Import → Import**
5. Select your exported file — all your data will be restored

The export file is the same on both platforms, so you can also move your data **from iPhone to Android** and **from Android to iPhone**.

> **Important:** Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all your data from the device. Make sure to export your data first if you want to keep it.
