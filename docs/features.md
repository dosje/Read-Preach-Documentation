# Features

> Features marked *Available from …* need that app version or later. Update the app to get them. See [What's New](../whats-new/) for all versions.

## Report Tab

The Report tab is your daily ministry log. It tracks everything you need for your monthly service report.

### Daily Entry

For each day you can record:
- **Hours** — actual time spent in ministry
- **Planned hours** — time planned ahead
- **Placements** — literature or magazines shared
- **Bible studies** — conducted studies
- **Comments** — personal notes for the day

Tap a day in the calendar to switch between days. All data is saved automatically as you type.

### Calendar

The calendar shows the current month with visual indicators on days that have data. You can:
- Tap any day to view or edit that day's entry
- Swipe left/right to navigate between months
- Tap the month/year title to jump to any month quickly
- Tap **Today** to return to the current date

On iPhone the calendar can be collapsed to a compact week view. On iPad it is always visible — as a sidebar in landscape, or above the form in portrait.

On Android tablets the Report tab opens with the month calendar. In landscape a navigation rail replaces the bottom tab bar; you can place it on the left or right in **Settings → General**.

### Monthly Summary

Below the daily form, a summary card shows:
- Total hours this month vs. your monthly goal
- Hours remaining to reach your goal
- Total placements and Bible studies for the month
- Year-to-date totals and yearly goal

### Role Management

In **Settings → Report Settings** you can configure your ministry role:

- **Recurring role** — set Publisher, Auxiliary Pioneer, or Regular Pioneer with a start month and optional end month. Each role carries a different monthly hour goal.
- **One-time assignment** — set a temporary Auxiliary Pioneer month (15 or 30 hours) for a single month without changing your recurring role.
- **Role history** — a log of all past role changes.

### Credit Hours

*Available from iOS 1.6.0 · Android 1.1.0*

Regular Pioneers can record credit hours. They count towards the hour goal and are shown separately from ministry hours.

### Bible Students

*Available from iOS 1.6.0 · Android 1.1.0*

Keep a list of your Bible students and tick the ones you studied with each day. Each Bible student counts once per month, so the monthly summary shows your number of **different** Bible studies (tap it to see the names). This number is also used in the shared report, the service year overview and the PDF.

- Add a Bible student in **Settings → Report Settings → Bible students**, or with **+ Add Bible student** directly in the daily report
- A Bible student you never ticked on any day can be deleted (with confirmation). Bible students already used in your reports can only be archived, so past months stay correct *(iOS 1.7.0 · Android 1.1.1)*
- Without active Bible students you can still count Bible studies with the counter; a hint reminds you that adding a Bible student counts them by name

Bible student names stay on your device and in your backup file. They are never included in a shared report, the PDF or a report sent to NW Publisher.

### Service Year Overview

*Available from iOS 1.5.0 · Android 1.0.0*

Tap **View service year** at the bottom of the monthly summary card to open the Service Year Overview.

- Use the picker at the top to select any service year (September–August) for which you have data
- The table shows all 12 months with the role you had that month, your hours, placements, and Bible studies
- Future months are shown with dashes and greyed out
- An orange **!** icon appears next to any past month where a pioneer goal was not reached (Auxiliary Pioneer and one-time assignments only — Regular Pioneer tracks the full year)
- The goal section below the table shows:
  - **Auxiliary Pioneer / one-time assignment** — the monthly goal for that month
  - **Regular Pioneer** — the full yearly total vs. the 600-hour annual goal
  - **Publisher** — no goal section shown
- Tap the share button (top right) to export the year as a PDF and open the share sheet (save to Files or Google Drive, print, or share via any app)

### Share Report

*Available from iOS 1.5.0 · Android 1.0.0*

Tap **Share report** at the bottom of the monthly summary card to send your monthly report as a text message.

- The message is role-aware:
  - **Pioneer or one-time assignment** — includes your hours and Bible studies for the month
  - **Publisher (no goal)** — simply states "Ministry done: yes"
- The system share sheet opens, so you can send via Messages, WhatsApp, email, or any other app

### Send to NW Publisher

*Available from iOS 1.6.0 · Android 1.1.0*

Send last month's report to the NW Publisher app on the same device:

1. Go to last month in the Report tab and tap **Send to NW Publisher** in the monthly summary
2. Check the values: **I shared in the ministry this month**, different Bible studies, whole hours (pioneers), credit (Regular Pioneers) and an optional **note for the secretary**
3. If there are leftover minutes, you can move them to the 1st of the current month
4. Tap **Open NW Publisher**: NW Publisher opens with its report form filled in. Check the values there and tap **Submit**

The report goes only to the installed NW Publisher app, never to a website, and Bible student names are never sent. If NW Publisher is not installed, nothing is sent.

**Reminder banner** *(iOS 1.7.0 · Android 1.1.1)*
From the 1st to the 10th of each month, the Report tab shows a banner such as **"October report · Send to NW Publisher"** if last month has data. Tap it to open the dialog for last month. The banner disappears once you have opened NW Publisher for that month. Tap **✕** to hide it for this month (you can undo this right away), or turn it off completely with **Settings → Report Settings → NW Publisher reminder**.

## Ministry Timer

*Available from iOS 1.3.3 · Android 1.0.0*

The Ministry Timer tracks how long you spend in field ministry and lets you save the time directly to your daily report.

### How to use

Tap the timer button in the Report tab to open the Ministry Timer:
- **Start** — begins timing when you enter the territory
- **Pause / Resume** — pause the timer during breaks and resume when you continue
- **Stop** — ends the session; a save dialog shows your time rounded to the nearest 30 minutes, ready to add to the day's report entry

### Home screen widgets — iPhone & iPad

Add a Ministry Timer widget to your home screen. Two sizes are available:
- **Small** — shows the current timer with icon-only Pause/Resume and Stop controls
- **Medium** — shows the timer on the left with labelled Pause, Resume, and Stop buttons on the right

Both sizes update automatically and let you control the timer without opening the app.

### Live Activity & Dynamic Island — iPhone

When you start the timer, a Live Activity is created automatically on supported devices:
- **Lock Screen** — shows the elapsed time with Pause/Resume and Stop controls
- **Dynamic Island** (iPhone 14 Pro and later) — shows a compact animated timer in the pill; long-press to expand and access controls

### Widget & notification — Android

- **Home screen widget** — shows the running time with a Pause/Resume button. The widget is resizable; tap it to open the app.
- **Timer notification** — while the timer runs, a notification shows the elapsed time with **Pause/Resume** and **Stop**. Stop opens the app so you can save the time to your report.

## Bible Tab

The Bible tab tracks your progress through all 1,189 chapters of the Bible.

### Progress Card

At the top of the Bible tab, the Progress card shows:
- A circular progress indicator with the percentage of chapters read
- Total chapters read out of 1,189
- Estimated completion date based on your reading plan
- Current reading streak (if the streak feature is enabled)
- A **View History** link to open your full reading history

### Today's Reading

If you have an active reading plan, a highlighted section shows today's assigned chapters. Each chapter card has:
- A checkbox to mark it as read (tap)
- A link button to open the chapter on JW.org (tap the arrow icon)

### Book List and Chapter Grid

The left column lists all Bible books with their completion percentage. Tap a book to open its chapter grid on the right. In the chapter grid:
- **Blue with checkmark** — chapter read
- **Green outline** — today's assigned chapter
- **Grey** — unread chapter

Tap a chapter to mark it read or unread. Long-press (or use the context menu) to open it in JW.org or JW Library.

### Reading Plans

Available reading modes:
- **Sequential** — Genesis to Revelation in order
- **NT → OT** — New Testament first, then Old Testament
- **Custom** — drag and drop books into your own preferred order
- **Free mode** — no daily schedule; mark chapters at your own pace

Available durations: 1 year, 2 years, 3 years, or a custom chapters-per-day rate (up to 10 per day).

If you fall behind, the app automatically reschedules missed chapters into your upcoming days while keeping the same daily pace.

### Reading Streak

*Available from iOS 1.2.0 · Android 1.0.0*

The streak counter tracks how many consecutive days you have read at least one chapter.

- A **2-day grace period** applies before the streak resets — if you miss a day, the streak stays active for two more days
- If the Daily Reminder is enabled, you receive a notification on the first and second missed day
- **Milestone celebrations** with confetti and haptic feedback appear at: 5, 14, 30, 90, 180, and 365 consecutive days
- A special full-screen celebration appears when you complete all 1,189 chapters

### Reading History

*Available from iOS 1.2.0 · Android 1.0.0*

Tap **View History** in the Progress card to open the history sheet. It shows:
- **Bible completions** — a log of every date you finished all 1,189 chapters (for tracking progress across multiple read-throughs over the years)
- **Daily reading log** — every chapter you have read, grouped by date (newest first), in Bible order

### Reading Quiz

*Available from iOS 1.3.0 · Android 1.0.0*

After marking today's chapters as read, a **Quiz** button appears in the Today's Reading section. The quiz tests your comprehension of the chapters you have just read.

- Questions are drawn from the chapters read today
- Works in all reading modes, including Free mode
- Tap **Start Quiz** to begin; swipe or tap to move between questions
- Each question shows the Bible chapter reference it is drawn from
- Your score is shown at the end of the session

The quiz is optional — you can skip it at any time.

**Quiz replay via long-press** *(iOS 1.6.0 · Android 1.0.0)*
Long-press any read chapter in the chapter grid to quiz it directly — without needing to finish today's reading plan first. Each chapter can earn points once per reading program cycle. Once a chapter has already been quizzed this cycle, the same long-press starts a replay session that shows your score but does not award points. When you complete the full reading program (all 1,189 chapters), all chapters unlock again for a new cycle.

### Memorial Tracker

During the Memorial season (approximately one week before and after Nisan 14), a Memorial card and daily reading section appear. These show the assigned passages from the Bible and the book *Jesus — The Way, the Truth, the Life* for each day leading up to and following the Memorial.

- Tap a passage to mark it as read
- Tap the link icon to open the passage on JW.org or in JW Library
- The Memorial card shows your overall progress through all passages
- The tracker resets automatically each year after the Memorial date

## Settings

### General Settings

- **Name** — your name, used in the PDF service year report and the monthly share message. Synced via iCloud if iCloud Sync is enabled.
- **Language** — English, German, or French. Changing the language restarts the app.
- **Haptic feedback** — enable or disable vibration feedback when tapping
- **iCloud Sync** *(iPhone & iPad)* — optionally sync data automatically across devices on the same Apple ID (off by default)
- **App icon** *(iPhone & iPad)* — choose from Default, Green, Gold, Purple, or Red
- **Navigation rail side** *(Android tablets)* — place the navigation rail on the left or right in landscape
- **Export & Import** *(Android)* — see [Export & Import](#export--import) below

### Report Settings

- **Date format** — choose how dates are displayed, with a live preview
- **Recurring role** — configure your ongoing ministry role and monthly hour goal
- **One-time assignment** — add a temporary pioneer month
- **Role history** — view and manage past role entries
- **Bible students** — add, rename, archive or delete Bible students
- **NW Publisher reminder** — show the banner to send last month's report during the first 10 days of a month (on by default)

### Bible Reading Settings

- **Bible language** — choose the language for JW.org links (defaults to app language)
- **Reading mode** — sequential, NT→OT, custom, or free
- **Reading duration** — 1, 2, 3 years or custom chapters per day
- **Custom book order** — drag books to reorder (available in Custom mode)
- **Generate plan / Recalculate** — create or regenerate your reading schedule
- **Daily reminder** — set a push notification time; the notification lists today's unread chapters
- **Reading streak** — toggle the streak tracker on or off
- **Quiz difficulty** — filter questions by difficulty: Mix (all levels), Easy, Medium, or Hard
- **Max quiz questions** — set the maximum number of questions per session (1–10)

### Memorial Tracker Settings

- **Enable Memorial Tracker** — show or hide the Memorial feature
- **Always show** — show the Memorial card outside the seasonal window
- **Memorial reminder** — set a daily notification during the Memorial season

### Export & Import

All your data can be exported to a JSON file and imported on another device or after reinstalling. On Android, Export & Import is found under **Settings → General**.
- Choose what to include: Ministry Reports, Bible Reading (chapters, plan, streak, history), Memorial Reading
- Export saves a file you can store anywhere (iCloud Drive, Files app, Google Drive, email, etc.)
- Import restores all selected data from a previously exported file
- The file format is the same on iPhone, iPad and Android, so you can move your data between platforms

## Privacy & Data

- **Your data stays private** — nothing is sent to any server or third party; no tracking or analytics
- **No account required** — the app works fully offline from first launch
- **Optional iCloud sync** *(iPhone & iPad)* — enable in Settings to automatically sync your data across all your devices on the same Apple ID; disabled by default
- **Android backup** — the Android app has no built-in sync; if Google backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android may include the app's data in your device backup
- **Export/Import** — back up and restore all your data manually as a JSON file
